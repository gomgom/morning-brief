Morning Maru Font Modification Log
==================================

2026
----

- Source font: MaruBuri SemiBold, distributed under the SIL Open Font
  License, Version 1.1.
- The bundled webfont is a reduced subset prepared for the Morning Brief
  standalone HTML layout.
- The font family's internal and display names were changed to "Morning Maru"
  to avoid use of the original Reserved Font Name in a modified font.
- The modified font is embedded as WOFF2 data inside fonts-embedded.css.
- This distribution is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by
  the original font author or distributor.
- The original license notice is preserved in MaruBuri-OFL.txt and is also
  embedded into generated HTML by scripts/inline-font-css.mjs.
